## ✦ How I build

Most of my repositories are active experiments rather than polished final products. I use them to work through real product questions: what the user is trying to do, where the workflow breaks, what belongs in the interface, what should be automated and what still needs a human decision.

I enjoy building the full path—from the first interaction to backend rules, data and deployment—but I am most interested in the point where product design and intelligent systems meet.

<table>
<tr>
<td width="33%" align="center">
<h3>◈ Product</h3>
<p>Start from the user journey, make the hard workflow visible, then reduce friction without hiding important decisions.</p>
</td>
<td width="33%" align="center">
<h3>◉ Intelligence</h3>
<p>Shape context, state and outputs so AI becomes a dependable product capability instead of a decorative chatbot.</p>
</td>
<td width="33%" align="center">
<h3>⌘ Systems</h3>
<p>Connect interfaces, services, data, security boundaries, testing and deployment into one understandable system.</p>
</td>
</tr>
</table>

## ⚡ Product first. AI with a reason.

I care less about attaching an AI label and more about making intelligence useful, inspectable and safe enough to belong inside a real workflow.

- 🧭 conversational discovery and intent-driven journeys;
- 🧩 structured outputs instead of fragile free-form text;
- 🔁 stateful interactions that recover, ask and continue;
- 📏 rubric-grounded feedback and human-in-the-loop assessment;
- 🔎 retrieval, context design, evaluation and observability;
- 🛟 uncertainty, failure recovery and clear human control.
