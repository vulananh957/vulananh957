## How I work

Most of my repositories are active experiments rather than polished final products. I use them to work through real product questions: what the user is trying to do, where the workflow breaks, what belongs in the interface, what should be automated and what still needs a human decision.

I enjoy building the full path—from the first interaction to backend rules, data and deployment—but I am most interested in the point where product design and intelligent systems meet.

<table>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top">
<strong>01 / Product</strong><br><br>
Start from the user journey. Make the hard workflow visible, then reduce friction without hiding important decisions.
</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">
<strong>02 / Intelligence</strong><br><br>
Shape context, state and outputs so AI becomes a dependable capability—not a decorative chatbot.
</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

Connect interface, services, data, security, testing and deployment into one understandable system.

## Applied AI, without the theatre

The interesting work is not attaching an AI label. It is deciding what the model should understand, what shape its output must take, how the surrounding product handles state and where uncertainty or human review belongs.

<code>intent-driven workflows</code> · <code>structured outputs</code> · <code>context design</code> · <code>retrieval</code> · <code>evaluation</code> · <code>observability</code> · <code>human-in-the-loop</code> · <code>failure recovery</code>
